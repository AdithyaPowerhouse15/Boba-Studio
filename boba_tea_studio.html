How Is Boba Made?

From chewy tapioca pearls to delicious milk tea,
discover the magic behind everyone's favourite drink!

🥛 What Do We Need?

⚫

Tapioca Pearls

The chewy balls that make boba special.

🍵

Tea

Black tea or green tea for the base.

🥛

Milk

Adds a creamy and smooth texture.

🍯

Sugar Syrup

Provides the sweetness and flavour.

🧊

Ice Cubes

Keeps the drink cool and refreshing.

👨‍🍳 How Is It Prepared?

01. Prepare the Tapioca Pearls

Boil tapioca pearls in water according to their package instructions until soft and chewy.

02. Brew the Tea

Steep black tea leaves in hot water and allow the tea to cool.

03. Add Sweetness

Mix sugar syrup into the brewed tea to achieve your preferred sweetness.

04. Mix the Milk

Add milk to the tea and stir until the mixture becomes smooth and creamy.

05. Assemble Your Boba!

Put the cooked tapioca pearls into a glass, add ice, and pour in the milk tea.

🧋

Your Boba Is Ready!

Chewy pearls + creamy milk tea = pure happiness.
